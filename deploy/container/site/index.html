    Services & Methodology

    Multi-Signal Detection

    1. Your clip arrives

      It goes to the analysis server, and is deleted the moment the verdict comes back. Nothing is kept.

    2. Snapshots, not the whole film

      Thirty moments are taken from across the clip, start to end. Checking every single frame would be slower without being wiser.

    3. Find the face

      Each snapshot is searched for a face and cropped to it. The face is where manipulation happens, so it is the only thing examined.

    4. Score each one

      Every cropped face is compared against thousands of real and generated faces the system has studied, and given its own score.

    5. Look for a pattern

      Scattered odd frames are usually noise. A long unbroken run of them is not, so the two are weighed differently.

    6. Answer, or say it cannot

      If too few snapshots held a face, you get not enough to judge rather than a guess dressed up as an answer.

    Known Limits

    Full Transparency

    Novel Generative Models

    Unseen architecture types may lower detection confidence. Unrecognized patterns are treated as unresolved rather than clean.

    Heavy Re-compression

    Social platform re-uploads erase fine-grained camera noise, which makes a clip harder to judge. This is not currently detected, so a heavily re-compressed video is scored as if it were untouched.

    Micro-Edits

    A short edit inside a long video can be averaged away by the clip score. Check the timeline bars rather than relying on the single number alone.
